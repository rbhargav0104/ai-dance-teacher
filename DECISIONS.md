- 2026-11-25 — Dancer selection: start on the largest body, then nearest hip midpoint; a detection > 0.2 (image-normalized) away is treated as someone else (frame left empty), re-acquire after 1 s missing. — "Do not silently switch identities."

- 2026-11-25 — `low_tracking_confidence` warning uses the confidence of joints actually measured; out-of-frame joints are reported as `cropped_body`/excluded instead. — Otherwise every thighs-up clip would be mislabelled noisy.
